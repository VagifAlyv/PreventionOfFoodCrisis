Forecasting hunger where monitoring has gone dark

In 2025 the main famine early-warning system went offline for months after its funding stopped. This tool forecasts, four months ahead, how much of each region in Ethiopia, Somalia and South Sudan will be in crisis (IPC phase 3 or worse), using only data nobody has to go and collect: satellite rainfall, market prices and conflict records. Every score below is from real data the model never saw while training.
